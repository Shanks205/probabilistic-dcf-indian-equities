## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Sharda Cropchem's market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the current market price may be assuming a stronger agrochemical-cycle recovery, better working-capital release, higher margin normalization, stronger registration-led growth, or a lower risk profile than the current conservative DCF framework assumes.

The research value of Sharda Cropchem is that it demonstrates how free cash flow can differ sharply from accounting profits when working capital is material. A probabilistic DCF framework makes that uncertainty visible by varying revenue growth, EBIT margin, capex, working capital, and WACC.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for Sharda Cropchem are:

1. working-capital intensity,
2. normalized EBIT margin,
3. agrochemical demand recovery,
4. product-registration pipeline,
5. export/geography mix,
6. capex and registration-related spending,
7. WACC / risk premium,
8. terminal growth assumptions.
