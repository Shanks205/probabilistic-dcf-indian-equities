# Sharda Cropchem Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Sharda Cropchem probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agrochemical cycle | Demand and pricing can vary by crop cycle, geography, and inventory destocking | Changes revenue growth and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through wide revenue and margin ranges | Level 2 draft |
| Export/geography mix | Europe and other export markets can affect realization and growth | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured in base/bull/downside assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Product registration pipeline | Registration investments support future growth but require spending and time | Changes growth and reinvestment | Revenue growth, capex / sales | Captured through capex and growth assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Receivables and inventory can absorb cash in agrochemical cycles | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through high NWC range | Level 2 draft |
| Gross margin recovery | Margin recovery may be slower if pricing pressure remains | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through downside margin case | Level 2 draft |
| Currency risk | Export revenues and imports may create FX sensitivity | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Qualitative risk; explicit FX model deferred | Level 2 draft |
| Capex/registration spend | Product registrations and business reinvestment can be high | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through higher capex assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Discount rate | Export/agrochemical cyclicality and working-capital risk raise required return | Reduces present value | WACC | Captured through higher WACC and CSRP | Level 2 draft |
| Valuation risk | Market price may imply faster recovery than conservative DCF assumptions | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Sharda Cropchem is a useful case because working-capital intensity can make accounting profits different from free cash flow. The model therefore emphasizes cash conversion and normalized working-capital assumptions.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
