# Sharda Cropchem Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Sharda Cropchem should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because agrochemical businesses differ by product portfolio, registration pipeline, geography mix, working-capital cycle, margin structure, and balance-sheet profile.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| UPL | Global agrochemical company | Global crop-protection benchmark | Leverage, scale, and integration differ materially |
| PI Industries | Agrochemical / CSM | High-quality agrochemical benchmark | CSM/export model differs from Sharda's registration-led model |
| Rallis India | Crop protection | Domestic/export agrochemical comparison | Tata group profile and product mix differ |
| Dhanuka Agritech | Agrochemical formulations | Domestic formulation peer | Geography and business model differ |
| Sumitomo Chemical India | Agrochemical and crop protection | Large listed agrochemical peer | Parentage and portfolio differ |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare agrochemical cycle recovery |
| EBITDA margin | Compare profitability and margin recovery |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Net working capital / sales | Compare cash-conversion burden |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Registration portfolio
Geography mix
Inventory and receivable cycle
Leverage
Margin recovery stage
Export exposure
Currency risk
```
