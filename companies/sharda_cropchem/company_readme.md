# Sharda Cropchem — Company 5

## Role in the Project

Sharda Cropchem is the fifth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the working-capital-sensitive agrochemical archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Agrochemicals / working-capital-sensitive global registration business
```

## Why Sharda Requires Different Treatment

Sharda Cropchem should not be valued using a simple generic DCF because its performance can be affected by global agrochemical demand, pricing cycles, product registration investments, inventory movement, receivables, geography mix, and gross-margin recovery.

The model therefore emphasizes normalized operating margins and working-capital intensity.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. global agrochemical demand recovery,
2. Europe and export geography mix,
3. gross-margin normalization,
4. product registration pipeline,
5. receivable and inventory working-capital intensity,
6. capex/product-registration spend,
7. WACC and terminal growth.
