# JB Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the JB Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic formulation growth | Growth depends on branded formulations, chronic therapies, prescription strength and market-share execution | Changes revenue growth and margin durability | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through revenue-growth and margin range | Level 2 draft |
| International formulations | Export markets can support growth but may carry pricing, demand and currency risk | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured qualitatively and in growth range | Level 2 draft |
| CDMO / contract manufacturing | CDMO contribution may improve scale but depends on client pipeline and execution | Affects growth visibility and asset utilization | Revenue growth, capex / sales | Captured through growth and capex assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Regulatory and compliance risk | Pharma businesses face plant, product, regulatory and quality-compliance risk | Can affect revenue, margins and WACC | Revenue growth, EBIT margin, WACC | Captured through CSRP and qualitative risk notes | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | High branded-formulation margins may normalize if product mix, price controls or competition changes | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Acquisition integration | Acquired brands can support growth but may carry integration and execution risk | Changes growth, margins and working capital | Revenue growth, EBIT margin, NWC / sales | Captured through CSRP and growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Pharma inventory, receivables and channel stocking can absorb cash | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may imply stronger defensive compounding than the base DCF | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

JB Chemicals is a useful defensive pharma case because a stable branded-generics business can have strong earnings quality, but the DCF must test whether the market price already discounts those defensive characteristics.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
