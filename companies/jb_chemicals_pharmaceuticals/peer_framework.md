# JB Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals Peer Framework

## Purpose

This file defines the relative-valuation peer framework for JB Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals. It is not a final peer valuation table. It is a Level 2 framework that will later support SSRN-stage peer multiple refresh and citation work.

## Business Context

JB Chemicals operates in branded formulations, domestic pharma, international formulations, and selected contract-development / manufacturing opportunities. Its valuation should be compared with pharma companies that have stable earnings, branded generics exposure, strong margins, and defensive cash-flow characteristics.

## Indicative Peer Buckets

| Peer Bucket | Relevance | Example Peer Types |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic branded formulation companies | Closest business-quality comparison | Indian pharma companies with branded domestic portfolios |
| Midcap pharma compounders | Similar market-cap and growth-profile comparison | Midcap pharma businesses with strong margins and branded exposure |
| Export-oriented formulation companies | International market comparison | Companies with formulation exports and regulated / semi-regulated market exposure |
| Defensive healthcare compounders | Valuation-quality comparison | Healthcare businesses with steady earnings and lower cyclicality |

## Multiples to Track Later

| Multiple | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| EV / Sales | Useful when margins differ across pharma peers |
| EV / EBITDA | Main operating valuation multiple for pharma companies |
| P / E | Captures market expectations for earnings growth and quality |
| P / B | Useful where ROE and capital efficiency are important |
| ROCE and ROE | Helps judge whether premium multiples are supported by returns |
| Revenue CAGR | Helps compare growth durability across peers |
| EBITDA margin | Helps compare operating quality and product mix |

## Peer-Selection Caution

A strict peer comparison may be difficult because JB Chemicals has a mix of domestic branded formulations, international formulations, acquired brands, and defensive pharma characteristics. Peer valuation should therefore be used as a secondary benchmark, not as the primary valuation method.

## SSRN Upgrade Requirement

During the SSRN stage, this framework should be upgraded with:

1. exact peer list,
2. consistent data date,
3. market capitalization,
4. enterprise value,
5. revenue, EBITDA, PAT,
6. EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/B,
7. ROCE, ROE,
8. source citations.

## Research Boundary

This peer framework is for research workflow design only. It is not investment advice or a final relative valuation conclusion.
