# Polycab India Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Polycab India should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because cables, wires, FMEG, EPC, and electricals businesses differ by product mix, distribution strength, working-capital cycle, margin profile, growth runway, and brand strength.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| KEI Industries | Wires and cables | Closest listed cable/wire peer | Scale, margin profile, and product mix differ |
| RR Kabel | Wires, cables, electricals | Listed wires/cables and FMEG reference | Recent listing and business mix differences |
| Havells India | Electricals/FMEG | Consumer electricals and distribution benchmark | Larger consumer brand mix differs |
| Finolex Cables | Cables | Mature cable peer | Growth and product mix differ |
| V-Guard Industries | Electricals | Consumer electricals reference | Product and regional mix differ |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare wires/cables/electricals growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Working capital / sales | Compare cash-conversion burden |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Cable/wire mix
FMEG exposure
EPC exposure
Distribution strength
Capex cycle
Working-capital intensity
Brand premium
```
