# Polycab India — Company 8

## Role in the Project

Polycab India is the eighth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the quality compounder / cables and electricals archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Cables and electricals / quality compounder / premium valuation risk
```

## Why Polycab Requires Different Treatment

Polycab should not be valued like a generic manufacturing company. Its valuation depends on wires and cables market leadership, distribution strength, FMEG turnaround, EPC execution, exports, operating margin durability, capex, working capital, and premium market expectations.

The model therefore emphasizes growth durability, margin sustainability, reinvestment intensity, working-capital discipline, and valuation-expectation risk.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. wires and cables growth,
2. FMEG margin improvement,
3. EPC execution and order visibility,
4. operating margin sustainability,
5. capex intensity,
6. working-capital intensity,
7. WACC and terminal growth,
8. premium valuation expectations.
