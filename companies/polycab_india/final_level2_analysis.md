## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Polycab India's market price is far above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the market price may be incorporating much stronger assumptions than the current DCF framework, such as a longer high-growth runway, superior margin expansion, FMEG turnaround, better working-capital efficiency, lower risk perception, or a longer competitive advantage period.

The research value of Polycab is that it demonstrates how a high-quality compounder can still have a large gap between business quality and cash-flow-supported valuation. A probabilistic DCF framework helps separate business excellence from price-implied expectations.
