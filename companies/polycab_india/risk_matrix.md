# Polycab India Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Polycab India probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wires and cables growth | Growth depends on housing, infra, capex, electrification, and distribution strength | Changes revenue growth and terminal scale | Revenue growth | Captured through revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| FMEG turnaround | Consumer electricals improvement can support margins but execution risk remains | Changes revenue and margin | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through margin and growth assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| EPC execution | EPC can add growth but may carry execution and margin variability | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Qualitative risk embedded in assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Copper/raw-material volatility | Input costs and pass-through timing affect margins and working capital | Changes EBIT and cash conversion | EBIT margin, NWC / sales | Captured through margin and NWC ranges | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Inventory and receivables can absorb cash in fast growth periods | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Capacity expansion and backward integration may require reinvestment | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may imply a long high-growth runway and durable margin expansion | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Competition | Cable/electricals market competition may affect pricing and margins | Reduces revenue growth and EBIT margin | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured in downside cases | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Polycab is a useful quality-compounder case because the business has strong growth and brand/distribution advantages, but the market valuation can still require assumptions far above a conservative DCF base case.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
