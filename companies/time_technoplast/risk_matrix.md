# Time Technoplast Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Time Technoplast probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw-material volatility | Polymer and input cost changes can affect spreads and margins | Changes EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Industrial demand | Packaging demand depends on industrial activity and end-market growth | Changes revenue growth | Revenue growth | Captured through bear/base/bull revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Value-added product mix | Higher value-added product contribution can improve margins and growth | Increases EBIT margin and terminal assumptions | EBIT margin, terminal growth | Captured through upside margin case | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Receivables and inventory can absorb cash | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Expansion and product-mix upgrades may require reinvestment | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex range | Level 2 draft |
| Debt and finance cost | Higher leverage or interest costs can affect equity value | Reduces equity value / raises risk | Net debt, WACC | Net debt and WACC included | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | Operating margins may normalize if input-cost pass-through weakens | Reduces FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through downside margin case | Level 2 draft |
| Valuation risk | Market price may imply stronger growth/margins than conservative DCF assumptions | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Time Technoplast is a useful industrial packaging case because accounting performance can be sensitive to raw-material spreads, working capital, and value-added product mix. The model tests whether conservative cash-flow assumptions support the market price.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
