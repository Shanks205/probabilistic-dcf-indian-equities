# Time Technoplast — Company 6

## Role in the Project

Time Technoplast is the sixth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the industrial packaging / polymer products archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Industrial packaging / polymer products / value-added product mix
```

## Why Time Technoplast Requires Different Treatment

Time Technoplast should not be treated exactly like an auto, commodity, or agrochemical business. Its valuation depends on industrial packaging demand, polymer/raw-material spreads, value-added product contribution, working capital, capacity expansion, and operating leverage.

The model therefore emphasizes margin stability, reinvestment, and working-capital sensitivity.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. industrial packaging demand,
2. polymer/raw-material cost volatility,
3. value-added product mix,
4. capacity utilization and operating leverage,
5. capex intensity,
6. working-capital intensity,
7. WACC and terminal growth.
