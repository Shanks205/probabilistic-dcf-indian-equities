## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Time Technoplast's market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the current market price may be assuming stronger margin expansion, higher value-added product mix, better working-capital efficiency, lower risk profile, or a longer growth runway than the current conservative DCF framework assumes.

The research value of Time Technoplast is that it demonstrates how industrial packaging valuation depends on raw-material pass-through, capex, leverage, and working-capital discipline. A probabilistic DCF framework makes these uncertainties explicit.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for Time Technoplast are:

1. EBIT margin sustainability,
2. raw-material spread and pass-through,
3. value-added product mix,
4. working-capital intensity,
5. capex intensity,
6. leverage and finance cost,
7. WACC / risk premium,
8. terminal growth assumptions.
