# Time Technoplast Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Time Technoplast should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because industrial packaging and polymer-product companies differ by product mix, value-added contribution, customer exposure, raw-material pass-through, leverage, and working-capital intensity.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mold-Tek Packaging | Packaging / containers | Listed packaging peer with margin comparison relevance | Product and end-market mix differ |
| EPL Limited | Packaging | Large listed packaging reference | Business mix and global exposure differ |
| Jindal Poly Films | Polymer products / packaging | Polymer spread and packaging reference | Film-cycle economics differ |
| Cosmo First | Specialty films / packaging | Packaging and value-added product comparison | Product mix differs materially |
| Supreme Industries | Plastic products | High-quality polymer/plastic products benchmark | Scale, brand, and product economics differ |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare industrial packaging demand growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Net debt / EBITDA | Compare balance-sheet risk |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Product mix
Raw-material pass-through
Value-added product share
Leverage
Working-capital intensity
Capacity expansion cycle
End-market exposure
```
