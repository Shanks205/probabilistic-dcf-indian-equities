# Hero MotoCorp Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Hero MotoCorp should not be valued by blindly copying peer multiples because business mix, export exposure, EV strategy, margin structure, and growth outlook differ across companies.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bajaj Auto | Two-wheelers and three-wheelers | Major Indian auto peer with strong export and margin profile | Export mix and premium motorcycle exposure differ from Hero |
| TVS Motor | Two-wheelers | Key domestic competitor with EV and premiumization exposure | Growth and margin structure may differ |
| Eicher Motors | Premium motorcycles / Royal Enfield | Useful for premiumization comparison | Not directly comparable to mass-market Hero |
| Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India | Two-wheelers | Direct competitive relevance | Not listed in India as a standalone comparable |
| Maruti Suzuki | Broader auto consumer demand sanity check | Useful for auto-cycle context | Four-wheeler business model differs |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare auto-cycle recovery |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Net cash / debt | Compare balance-sheet strength |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## How Peer Data Will Be Used

Peer data will be used to test whether Hero's DCF assumptions are reasonable:

```text
DCF implied EV/EBITDA
vs
Hero historical EV/EBITDA
vs
listed peer EV/EBITDA range
```

The peer framework will not determine fair value mechanically.
