## Why Hero MotoCorp Is Included

Hero MotoCorp represents the mature auto / two-wheeler recovery archetype. It is included because it provides a relatively clean first case for applying the project’s FCFF DCF and Monte Carlo framework before moving to more complex cyclical or order-book-driven businesses.

## Interpretation

The updated model shows that under the current conservative public-source DCF assumption set, Hero MotoCorp's simulated valuation range is materially below the market price used in the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means that the current DCF assumptions do not support the observed market price unless stronger growth, higher margins, lower discount rate, higher terminal value assumptions, or non-operating optionality are justified.

## Main Research Insight

The Hero MotoCorp case demonstrates why probabilistic DCF is useful. A single-point DCF would only show one fair value estimate. The Monte Carlo framework shows a full distribution and makes it clearer that the market price may require optimistic assumptions beyond the current base-case framework.
