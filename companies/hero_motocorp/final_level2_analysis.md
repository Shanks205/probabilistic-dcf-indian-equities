## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Hero MotoCorp's current market price is above the current conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not mean the stock is automatically overvalued. It means that the current market price requires one or more stronger assumptions than those used in this model, such as higher long-term growth, stronger margin expansion, lower cost of equity, greater EV optionality, stronger premiumization, or higher non-operating asset value.

The main research value of this company case is not the point estimate. The important finding is that Monte Carlo DCF makes valuation uncertainty visible. Instead of relying on a single target price, the model shows the range of plausible values and the probability that the intrinsic value exceeds the market price under the chosen assumptions.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for Hero MotoCorp are:

1. EBIT margin sustainability,
2. terminal growth,
3. WACC / cost of equity,
4. two-wheeler volume growth,
5. EV transition and reinvestment requirements,
6. working-capital intensity,
7. premiumization and product mix.

## Peer Sanity Check

Hero MotoCorp should be compared with Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, Eicher Motors, and broader auto-cycle references such as Maruti Suzuki. However, peers should only be used as a sanity check because each company differs in export mix, premiumization, EV exposure, margin profile, and product category.
