# Hero MotoCorp — Company 1

## Role in the Project

Hero MotoCorp is the first company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It is used as the initial implementation case because it is a mature auto/two-wheeler company with relatively clearer FCFF DCF logic than highly cyclical commodity or refining businesses.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Mature auto cash-flow recovery
```

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model will focus on the following uncertainty drivers:

1. Two-wheeler volume recovery
2. Revenue growth sustainability
3. EBITDA / EBIT margin stability
4. Premiumisation and product mix
5. EV transition risk
6. Competitive intensity
7. Capex intensity
8. Working capital requirements
9. WACC and terminal growth

## Initial Modelling Approach

The first version uses a simplified FCFF model with placeholder assumptions. These assumptions are not final and must be replaced after audited financial statement reconciliation and updated market input verification.
