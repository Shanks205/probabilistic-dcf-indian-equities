# Hero MotoCorp Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Hero MotoCorp probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand cycle | Two-wheeler volumes may recover slower or faster than expected | Changes revenue growth and operating leverage | Revenue growth | Captured through bear/base/bull revenue growth range | Preliminary |
| Premiumisation | Premium motorcycle and scooter mix may improve margins, but execution may vary | Changes EBIT margin and terminal growth expectations | EBIT margin, revenue growth | Captured through margin distribution | Preliminary |
| EV transition | Electric two-wheeler transition may require investment and may pressure legacy economics | Changes capex, margins, and terminal assumptions | Capex / sales, EBIT margin, terminal growth | Qualitative risk noted; detailed EV model pending | Incomplete |
| Competition | Competitive intensity from Bajaj, TVS, Honda, and EV players may pressure pricing | Reduces growth and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured in downside assumptions | Preliminary |
| Rural demand | Rural income, monsoon, financing availability, and replacement demand affect volumes | Changes revenue growth cyclicality | Revenue growth | Included in revenue-growth uncertainty | Preliminary |
| Raw material cost | Commodity and input cost changes can affect gross margin | Changes EBIT margin | EBIT margin | Captured in margin distribution | Preliminary |
| Capex risk | Higher EV/platform investment could increase reinvestment needs | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex range | Preliminary |
| Working capital | Inventory, dealer financing, receivables, and payables can affect cash conversion | Reduces or increases FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC range | Preliminary |
| Discount rate | Beta, ERP, and company-specific risk premium may differ from placeholder WACC | Changes PV of FCFF and terminal value | WACC | Placeholder WACC used; beta regression pending | Incomplete |
| Terminal value | Long-term growth and terminal margin assumptions dominate value | Changes terminal value significantly | Terminal growth, EBIT margin, WACC | Captured through terminal growth and WACC distribution | Preliminary |
| Data quality | Public-source numbers may differ from audited/reconciled model inputs | Could change all valuation outputs | All variables | Source log and audit checklist flag this risk | Incomplete |

## Key Research Point

Hero MotoCorp is not only a revenue-growth story. The valuation is sensitive to margin durability, reinvestment needs, EV transition costs, and the discount rate. For SSRN-level work, these risks must be translated into explicit assumption ranges rather than discussed only qualitatively.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
