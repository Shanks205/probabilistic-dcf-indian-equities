# Hero MotoCorp Annual Report Reconciliation Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist defines the official annual-report reconciliation work required before Hero MotoCorp can be marked as Level 2 Public-Source Research Complete.

The current model uses a public-source FY2024-FY2025 annual-report analysis baseline. Final SSRN-ready work requires line-by-line reconciliation against the official annual report and market data.

## Official Source Location

Hero MotoCorp publishes annual reports on its investor relations / annual report page. The FY2024-FY2025 annual report must be downloaded and used as the primary source for final reconciliation.

## Consolidated vs Standalone Rule

The project should use consolidated financial statements unless a clear reason exists to use standalone statements. If standalone numbers are used for any section, the source log must clearly explain why.

## Completion Rule

Hero MotoCorp cannot be marked Level 2 until every key item above is either:

1. reconciled directly to the annual report,
2. clearly sourced from another reliable public source, or
3. explicitly marked as unavailable and excluded from final conclusions.

## Research Boundary

This checklist is for research-quality control. It does not provide investment advice or a buy/sell recommendation.
