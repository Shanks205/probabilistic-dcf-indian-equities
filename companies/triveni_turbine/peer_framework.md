# Triveni Turbine Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Triveni Turbine should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because capital-goods companies differ by order-book quality, export mix, service revenue, margin durability, working-capital intensity, and capital intensity.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| TD Power Systems | Rotating electrical equipment / capital goods | Useful industrial equipment peer | Product mix differs from turbines |
| Siemens India | Capital goods / industrial automation | Large high-quality capital-goods benchmark | Scale and business mix differ materially |
| ABB India | Electrification and automation | Premium industrial capex benchmark | Not directly comparable by product |
| CG Power | Electrical equipment and industrial systems | Capital-goods cycle comparison | Turnaround/re-rating effects may distort multiples |
| Bharat Heavy Electricals | Power equipment | Broader power equipment context | State-owned and margin profile differs significantly |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare industrial capex/order conversion growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Order book / revenue | Compare growth visibility where available |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Order book visibility
Export mix
Aftermarket revenue
Margin profile
Working-capital requirements
Capital intensity
Net cash / debt profile
```
