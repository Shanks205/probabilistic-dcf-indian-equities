# Triveni Turbine Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Triveni Turbine probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Order-book conversion | Orders may not convert into revenue at expected pace | Changes revenue growth and working capital | Revenue growth, NWC / sales | Captured in revenue and NWC distributions | Level 2 draft |
| Export growth | Export demand can support growth but may be volatile | Changes revenue growth and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured in growth and margin ranges | Level 2 draft |
| Aftermarket mix | Higher services/aftermarket mix may improve margin durability | Changes EBIT margin and terminal assumptions | EBIT margin, terminal growth | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Industrial capex cycle | Capital-goods demand depends on broader industrial investment cycle | Changes order inflow and revenue | Revenue growth | Captured through bear/base/bull growth | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | High margins may normalize if mix or competition changes | Reduces FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Order execution and receivables can increase working-capital needs | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC range | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Capacity or technology investment may increase reinvestment | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex range | Level 2 draft |
| Valuation risk | Market price may imply stronger growth and longer competitive advantage period than current DCF | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Discount rate | WACC sensitivity matters because terminal value is material | Changes PV of terminal value | WACC | Captured through WACC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source and annual-report definitions may differ | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Triveni Turbine is a useful capital-goods case because order-book growth and high margins can support a premium market valuation, but the DCF model must test whether those expectations are already priced in.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
