## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Triveni Turbine's market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the market price requires stronger assumptions than the current base framework, such as a longer high-growth runway, sustained premium margins, lower discount rate, stronger export/aftermarket contribution, or higher terminal competitive advantage.

The research value of the Triveni case is that it demonstrates how order-book-driven capital-goods companies can command premium market valuations, but a DCF framework forces the analyst to test whether growth visibility and margin durability are sufficient to support those expectations.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for Triveni Turbine are:

1. revenue growth durability,
2. order-book conversion,
3. EBIT margin sustainability,
4. export and aftermarket mix,
5. working-capital intensity,
6. WACC / cost of equity,
7. terminal growth assumptions.
