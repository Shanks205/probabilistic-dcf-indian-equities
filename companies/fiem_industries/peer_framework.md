# Fiem Industries Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

Fiem Industries should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because auto-component companies differ by customer mix, product mix, EV exposure, margin structure, export mix, and capital intensity.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lumax Industries | Automotive lighting and components | Closest listed lighting/component peer | Customer mix and margin profile may differ |
| Minda Corporation | Auto components / electronics | Useful for component-sector valuation context | Broader component mix than Fiem |
| Uno Minda | Auto components / electronics | Large diversified auto-component benchmark | Scale and product diversity differ materially |
| Varroc Engineering | Auto components / lighting | Useful for lighting and export comparison | Balance-sheet and margin profile differ |
| Sona BLW Precision | Auto components / EV drivetrain | High-quality EV-linked component reference | Product economics differ strongly |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare supplier-cycle growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Net debt / cash | Compare balance-sheet strength |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Product mix
Customer concentration
EV exposure
Export exposure
Margin durability
Debt and working-capital profile
```
