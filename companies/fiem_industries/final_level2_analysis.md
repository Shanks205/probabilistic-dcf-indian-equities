## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Fiem Industries' current market price is far above the current conservative DCF valuation distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the market price requires materially stronger assumptions than the current base framework, such as higher long-term growth, higher margin durability, lower discount rate, stronger EV/product optionality, or a longer competitive advantage period.

The research value of the Fiem case is that it demonstrates how a high-quality midcap auto-component supplier can have a very different valuation uncertainty profile from a mature OEM. Monte Carlo DCF helps separate operational quality from price-implied expectations.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for Fiem Industries are:

1. revenue growth durability,
2. EBIT margin sustainability,
3. customer/platform concentration,
4. EV and LED content opportunity,
5. capex intensity,
6. working-capital intensity,
7. WACC / midcap risk premium,
8. terminal growth assumptions.
