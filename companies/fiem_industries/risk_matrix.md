# Fiem Industries Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Fiem Industries probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-wheeler cycle | Fiem is exposed to the health of the Indian two-wheeler industry | Changes revenue growth and operating leverage | Revenue growth | Captured in bear/base/bull revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Customer concentration | Major OEM exposure can create platform and order concentration risk | Can affect growth and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through wider midcap supplier assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| LED content growth | Higher lighting/electronics content per vehicle may support growth | Supports revenue growth and margin durability | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through base/bull assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| EV transition | EV platform adoption can create opportunity but also product-mix risk | Changes growth, capex, and margins | Revenue growth, capex / sales, EBIT margin | Qualitative risk noted; detailed segment model later | Level 2 draft |
| Raw material costs | Input cost volatility can pressure margins | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Auto-component suppliers can face receivable/inventory pressure | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Capacity expansion and tooling can increase reinvestment needs | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex range | Level 2 draft |
| Discount rate | Midcap risk, liquidity, customer concentration, and cyclicality raise cost of equity | Reduces present value and terminal value | WACC | WACC set above large-cap auto case | Level 2 draft |
| Valuation risk | Market valuation can price in stronger growth than conservative DCF assumptions | Creates gap between market price and intrinsic distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source and annual-report data definitions may differ | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Source log and final input table document status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Fiem Industries is a useful case because a high-quality auto-component supplier can look attractive operationally while still requiring careful valuation discipline. The model separates business quality from price-implied expectations.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
