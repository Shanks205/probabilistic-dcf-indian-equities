# Fiem Industries — Company 2

## Role in the Project

Fiem Industries is the second company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the auto-component growth archetype and adds customer/platform concentration risk, content-per-vehicle growth, and margin sustainability uncertainty to the project.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Auto-component growth / customer and platform concentration
```

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on the following uncertainty drivers:

1. two-wheeler industry volume growth,
2. LED lighting content per vehicle,
3. customer/platform concentration,
4. electric vehicle component opportunity,
5. operating margin sustainability,
6. capex intensity,
7. working capital intensity,
8. WACC and terminal growth.
