# Shilchar Technologies — Company 10

## Role in the Project

Shilchar Technologies is the tenth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the high-ROCE industrial growth / transformer manufacturing archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Transformer manufacturing / high-ROCE industrial growth / power capex cycle
```

## Why Shilchar Requires Different Treatment

Shilchar should not be valued like a mature industrial company. Its valuation depends on transformer demand from renewable energy, transmission and distribution capex, export opportunity, capacity expansion, order execution, receivable discipline, margin sustainability, and the risk that high growth and high ROCE may already be priced into the stock.

The model therefore emphasizes revenue-growth durability, operating-margin normalization risk, working-capital absorption, capex intensity, WACC, terminal growth, and premium valuation expectations.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. transformer demand and power capex cycle,
2. renewable-energy and grid investment demand,
3. export growth,
4. revenue growth durability,
5. operating margin sustainability,
6. receivable and inventory intensity,
7. capacity expansion and capex intensity,
8. WACC and terminal growth,
9. premium valuation expectations.
