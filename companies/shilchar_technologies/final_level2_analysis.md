## Company Role in the Research Sample

Shilchar Technologies is included as the high-ROCE industrial growth / transformer manufacturing case. It adds a different uncertainty profile because its valuation depends on transformer demand, renewable-energy and grid capex, export opportunity, capacity expansion, order execution, working-capital discipline, margin sustainability, and premium valuation expectations.

The research value of Shilchar is that it helps test whether a high-return industrial growth business can justify a premium market valuation under a probabilistic FCFF framework.

## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Shilchar Technologies' market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the business is weak. It means the market price may be discounting a stronger and longer growth runway than the current base-case FCFF framework, including sustained transformer demand, export expansion, high margins, strong ROCE, low debt, and a durable power-capex cycle.

The company is useful for the project because it shows how a high-quality industrial growth story can still carry valuation-expectation risk. A probabilistic DCF framework helps separate operating excellence from the price already being paid for that excellence.
