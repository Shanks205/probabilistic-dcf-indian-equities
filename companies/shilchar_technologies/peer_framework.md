# Shilchar Technologies Peer Framework

## Purpose

This file defines the relative-valuation peer framework for Shilchar Technologies. It is not a final peer valuation table. It is a Level 2 framework that will later support SSRN-stage peer multiple refresh and citation work.

## Business Context

Shilchar Technologies operates in transformer manufacturing and benefits from themes such as power transmission, renewable-energy infrastructure, grid investment, industrial electrification, and export demand.

Because the company has high profitability and growth characteristics, the peer set should not be limited only to generic electrical-equipment manufacturers. It should include companies exposed to transformers, electrical capital goods, power infrastructure, and high-quality industrial manufacturing.

## Indicative Peer Buckets

| Peer Bucket | Relevance | Example Peer Types |
|---|---|---|
| Transformer manufacturers | Closest operating comparison | Transformer-focused listed companies and electrical equipment manufacturers |
| Power T&D equipment | Similar end-market exposure | Transmission, distribution, grid-equipment and power-infrastructure companies |
| Electrical capital goods | Broader sector benchmark | Electrical industrials with capex-cycle exposure |
| High-ROCE industrial growth | Quality-growth valuation comparison | Midcap industrials with high margins, low debt, and strong return ratios |

## Multiples to Track Later

| Multiple | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| EV / Sales | Useful when margins differ materially across peers |
| EV / EBITDA | Main operating valuation multiple for capital goods and industrials |
| P / E | Captures market expectations for earnings growth and return profile |
| P / B | Useful when ROE and capital efficiency are important |
| ROCE and ROE | Helps judge whether premium multiples are supported by returns |
| Revenue CAGR | Helps compare growth durability across peers |
| EBITDA margin | Helps compare operating quality and product mix |

## Peer-Selection Caution

A strict peer comparison may be difficult because Shilchar's combination of high margins, low debt, transformer-cycle exposure, and growth may differ materially from larger diversified electrical-equipment companies. Therefore, peer valuation should be used as a secondary benchmark, not as the primary valuation method.

## SSRN Upgrade Requirement

During the SSRN stage, this framework should be upgraded with:

1. exact peer list,
2. consistent data date,
3. market capitalization,
4. enterprise value,
5. revenue, EBITDA, PAT,
6. EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/B,
7. ROCE, ROE,
8. source citations.

## Research Boundary

This peer framework is for research workflow design only. It is not investment advice or a final relative valuation conclusion.
