# Shilchar Technologies Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Shilchar Technologies probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power capex cycle | Transformer demand depends on transmission, distribution, renewable-energy and industrial capex cycles | Changes revenue growth and operating leverage | Revenue growth | Captured through revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Export growth | Export demand can support scale but may carry order, FX and customer concentration risk | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured qualitatively and in growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Capacity expansion | Growth may require capacity additions and execution discipline | Higher capex can reduce near-term FCFF but support future growth | Capex / sales, revenue growth | Captured through capex distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | High margins may normalize if product mix, competition or input costs change | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Order-based industrial businesses can absorb cash through receivables and inventory | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Customer concentration | Large orders or export customers may create revenue concentration risk | Raises forecast uncertainty and risk premium | Revenue growth, WACC | Captured through CSRP and qualitative risk notes | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may imply a longer high-growth runway than conservative DCF assumptions | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Shilchar Technologies is a useful high-ROCE industrial growth case because strong profitability and transformer-cycle tailwinds may justify business quality, but the DCF must test whether the market price already discounts a very long growth runway.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
