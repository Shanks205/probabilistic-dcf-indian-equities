# SJS Enterprises — Company 9

## Role in the Project

SJS Enterprises is the ninth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the auto ancillary / decorative aesthetics and self-adhesive labels archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Auto ancillary / decorative aesthetics / premiumization and content-per-vehicle growth
```

## Why SJS Requires Different Treatment

SJS should not be valued like a generic manufacturing company. Its valuation depends on automotive production growth, premiumization, content per vehicle, two-wheeler and passenger vehicle demand, export mix, margin sustainability, acquisitions, working-capital discipline, and midcap valuation expectations.

The model therefore emphasizes revenue growth durability, margin sustainability, capex, working capital, and customer/platform mix.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. two-wheeler and passenger vehicle production growth,
2. premiumization and content per vehicle,
3. customer/platform mix,
4. export growth,
5. operating margin sustainability,
6. capex intensity,
7. working-capital intensity,
8. WACC and terminal growth.
