# SJS Enterprises Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

SJS Enterprises should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because auto ancillary businesses differ by product category, customer mix, vehicle-segment exposure, export exposure, content-per-vehicle opportunity, margin profile, and capital intensity.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minda Corporation | Auto components and electronics | Broad auto ancillary peer | Product mix differs from decorative aesthetics |
| Uno Minda | Auto components and electronics | Large diversified auto-component benchmark | Scale and product diversity differ materially |
| Fiem Industries | Auto components / lighting | Auto ancillary comparison from project sample | Lighting economics differ from SJS |
| Lumax Industries | Automotive lighting/components | Auto-component peer | Product mix and margin profile differ |
| Sandhar Technologies | Auto components | Auto ancillary reference | Product category differs |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare auto ancillary growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Customer concentration | Compare business risk where available |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Product category
Customer/platform mix
Vehicle segment exposure
Export exposure
Margin profile
Capital intensity
Acquisition/integration effects
```
