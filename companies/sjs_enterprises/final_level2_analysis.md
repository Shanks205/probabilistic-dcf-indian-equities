## Company Role in the Research Sample

SJS Enterprises is included as the auto ancillary / decorative aesthetics and self-adhesive labels case. It adds a different uncertainty profile because its valuation depends on two-wheeler and passenger-vehicle production, premiumization, content per vehicle, customer/platform mix, export opportunity, acquisition integration, margin sustainability, capex, working capital, and midcap valuation expectations.

The research value of SJS is that it helps test whether a premium auto-ancillary business with strong margins and content-per-vehicle growth can justify its market-implied valuation under a probabilistic FCFF framework.

## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that SJS Enterprises' market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the company is poor quality or that the stock must decline. It means the market price appears to be discounting assumptions that are stronger than the current base-case FCFF framework, such as a longer premiumization runway, higher content per vehicle, stronger export growth, better acquisition integration, more durable margins, or a lower perceived risk profile.

The company is useful for the project because it separates business quality from valuation risk. SJS can be a strong operating business while still showing a large gap between current market price and conservative cash-flow-supported valuation.
