# SJS Enterprises Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the SJS Enterprises probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Auto production cycle | Demand depends on two-wheeler, passenger vehicle, and broader auto production volumes | Changes revenue growth and operating leverage | Revenue growth | Captured through revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Premiumization | Higher decorative/aesthetic content can increase revenue per vehicle | Supports growth and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through base/bull assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Customer/platform mix | Customer concentration or platform exposure can affect growth visibility | Changes revenue and risk profile | Revenue growth, WACC | Captured through CSRP and growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Export growth | Export opportunity can support growth but may carry demand and FX risk | Changes revenue and margins | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured qualitatively and in growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | High margins may normalize if competition or mix changes | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Acquisition/integration risk | Acquisitions can add growth but may affect margins, capex, and execution | Changes growth, margins, and reinvestment | Revenue growth, EBIT margin, capex / sales | Captured in CSRP and capex assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Auto ancillary receivables and inventory can absorb cash | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may imply stronger content growth and longer runway than base DCF | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

SJS Enterprises is a useful auto-ancillary case because premiumization and content-per-vehicle growth can justify quality characteristics, but the DCF must test whether the market price already discounts those expectations.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
