# NALCO Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the NALCO probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminium price cycle | Realizations depend heavily on aluminium and alumina prices | Changes revenue, margins, and FCFF | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured through mid-cycle margin normalization and wide margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Alumina margin cycle | Alumina prices and spreads can materially affect profitability | Changes operating margin | EBIT margin | Captured through margin range | Level 2 draft |
| Power and input costs | Energy, coal, caustic soda, freight, and raw-material costs affect margins | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through downside margin case | Level 2 draft |
| Production volume | Volume disruptions or ramp-up changes affect revenue and fixed-cost absorption | Changes revenue and margin | Revenue growth, EBIT margin | Captured in growth and margin range | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Mining, refining, smelting, and expansion capex can be heavy | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through high capex range | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Commodity price swings can affect inventory and receivables | Reduces or increases FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC range | Level 2 draft |
| PSU / policy risk | Government ownership, dividend policy, and capital allocation may affect valuation | Affects discount rate and cash returns | WACC, terminal assumptions | Captured through company-specific risk premium | Level 2 draft |
| Terminal value risk | Commodity businesses may not deserve high terminal growth/margins | Reduces terminal value | Terminal growth, EBIT margin | Terminal growth kept conservative | Level 2 draft |
| Market price risk | Market price may reflect peak-cycle earnings expectations or commodity momentum | Creates gap between market price and normalized DCF | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

NALCO is a strong test case because a commodity company can look optically cheap or expensive depending on where the cycle is. The model therefore uses normalized / mid-cycle assumptions instead of extrapolating peak-cycle margins.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
