## Company Role in the Research Sample

NALCO is included as the cyclical commodity / aluminium case. It adds a very different valuation problem from the auto and capital-goods companies because its reported earnings can be heavily influenced by aluminium prices, alumina spreads, energy costs, and commodity-cycle timing.

## Why Mid-Cycle Normalization Is Used

FY2025 was a strong profitability year for NALCO. A mechanical DCF that extrapolates peak-cycle earnings can overstate normalized intrinsic value. Therefore, this Level 2 draft model uses a normalized / mid-cycle EBIT margin rather than blindly carrying forward peak-cycle margins.

## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that NALCO's market price is materially above the conservative normalized DCF valuation distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the market price may be incorporating stronger aluminium-cycle assumptions, higher commodity prices, stronger cash generation, higher dividend expectations, lower risk perception, or a longer commodity upcycle than the current normalized model assumes.

The research value of the NALCO case is that it shows why commodity companies require cycle-aware valuation. A point-estimate DCF based only on a strong profit year can create false precision. A probabilistic DCF with normalized margins makes the commodity-cycle risk more visible.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for NALCO are:

1. aluminium and alumina price cycle,
2. normalized EBIT margin,
3. power and input costs,
4. capex intensity,
5. working-capital intensity,
6. WACC / commodity risk premium,
7. terminal growth assumptions,
8. dividend and capital-allocation expectations.
