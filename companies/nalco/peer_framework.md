# NALCO Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

NALCO should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because commodity companies differ by vertical integration, product mix, power costs, mining access, export exposure, balance-sheet strength, and cycle position.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hindalco Industries | Aluminium / metals | Large Indian aluminium and downstream peer | Much more diversified and globally exposed |
| Vedanta | Metals and mining | Aluminium and diversified commodity exposure | Conglomerate leverage and business mix differ materially |
| Hindustan Zinc | Mining / metals | High-margin metal producer sanity check | Zinc/silver economics differ from aluminium |
| MOIL | Mining PSU | PSU commodity reference | Manganese economics differ from aluminium |
| Steel Authority of India | PSU cyclical metals | PSU commodity-cycle reference | Steel economics differ from aluminium |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare commodity-cycle scale growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare cycle-adjusted profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Net cash / debt | Compare balance-sheet strength |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check, but cyclicality must be considered |
| Dividend yield | Important for PSU/commodity valuation context |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Commodity cycle position
Product mix
Integrated mining/refining/smelting economics
Power cost structure
Leverage and net cash
Dividend policy
Government ownership and capital allocation
```
