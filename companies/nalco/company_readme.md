# NALCO — Company 4

## Role in the Project

National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO) is the fourth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the cyclical commodity / aluminium archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Cyclical commodity / aluminium and alumina margin cycle
```

## Why NALCO Requires Different Treatment

NALCO should not be valued using a simple straight-line growth DCF because its revenue, margins, and earnings are strongly affected by aluminium prices, alumina prices, input costs, energy costs, and commodity-cycle conditions.

For this reason, the model uses a normalized / mid-cycle DCF approach rather than extrapolating FY2025 peak-cycle margins.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. aluminium and alumina prices,
2. commodity-cycle margins,
3. power and input costs,
4. production volumes,
5. export realization,
6. capex intensity,
7. working-capital intensity,
8. WACC and terminal growth.
