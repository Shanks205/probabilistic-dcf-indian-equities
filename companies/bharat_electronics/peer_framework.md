# Bharat Electronics Peer Framework

## Purpose

Peer comparison is used only as a sanity check. It does not replace intrinsic valuation.

BEL should not be valued by mechanically copying peer multiples because defence electronics, shipbuilding, aerospace, and defence manufacturing companies differ by order-book quality, execution cycle, margin profile, government exposure, working capital, and capital intensity.

## Suggested Peer Set

| Peer | Business Relevance | Why Included | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hindustan Aeronautics | Defence aerospace PSU | Large listed defence benchmark | Aerospace economics differ from electronics |
| Bharat Dynamics | Defence missiles and systems | Defence PSU order-book peer | Product economics differ materially |
| Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders | Defence shipbuilding PSU | Defence order-book and PSU valuation reference | Shipbuilding cycle differs strongly |
| Data Patterns | Defence electronics | Closer defence electronics peer | Scale and margin profile differ |
| Astra Microwave Products | Defence electronics / RF systems | Electronics and defence exposure | Smaller scale and different mix |

## Peer Metrics to Track

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Compare order execution and defence growth |
| EBITDA margin | Compare operating profitability |
| EBIT margin | Compare operating economics |
| ROCE | Compare capital efficiency |
| Order book / revenue | Compare revenue visibility |
| EV / EBITDA | Market-implied operating valuation |
| P/E | Earnings-multiple sanity check |
| FCF yield | Cash-flow valuation sanity check |

## Peer Use Rule

Peer multiples should only be interpreted after checking:

```text
Order-book visibility
Execution cycle
Government/customer concentration
Working-capital profile
Margin sustainability
Net cash / debt
PSU policy and capital allocation
```
