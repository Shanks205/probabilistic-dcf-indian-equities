## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that BEL's market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the stock is overvalued. It means the current market price may be incorporating a much stronger defence growth runway, superior order-book conversion, sustained premium margins, lower risk perception, or a longer competitive advantage period than the current DCF model assumes.

The research value of BEL is that it demonstrates how a high-quality defence compounder can have strong fundamentals but still require disciplined valuation. A probabilistic DCF framework makes the gap between cash-flow-supported value and market-implied expectations more visible.

## Key Assumption Drivers

The most important variables for BEL are:

1. defence order inflow,
2. order-book execution,
3. EBIT margin sustainability,
4. government-customer working-capital cycle,
5. capex intensity,
6. WACC / risk premium,
7. terminal growth assumptions,
8. premium valuation expectations.
