# Bharat Electronics Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Bharat Electronics probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Order inflow | Defence order inflow can be lumpy and policy-dependent | Changes revenue growth and execution visibility | Revenue growth | Captured through revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Order execution | Delays in execution or delivery can defer revenue recognition | Changes revenue and working capital | Revenue growth, NWC / sales | Captured through growth and NWC assumptions | Level 2 draft |
| Government-customer concentration | Defence/government exposure can create payment, tender, and policy risk | Affects growth, working capital, and risk premium | Revenue growth, NWC / sales, WACC | Captured through NWC and CSRP | Level 2 draft |
| Margin sustainability | High margins may normalize due to competition, mix, or cost pressure | Reduces EBIT and FCFF | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Government receivables and inventory requirements can absorb cash | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through high NWC range | Level 2 draft |
| Capex intensity | Defence electronics expansion may require reinvestment | Reduces FCFF | Capex / sales | Captured through capex range | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may price in a long defence growth runway | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| PSU/policy risk | Government ownership and policy direction can affect capital allocation | Affects WACC and terminal assumptions | WACC, terminal growth | Captured through CSRP | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

BEL is a useful case because a high-quality defence electronics company may deserve a premium, but the DCF must test whether order-book growth and margin durability are sufficient to support that premium.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
