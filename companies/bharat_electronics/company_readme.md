# Bharat Electronics — Company 7

## Role in the Project

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) is the seventh company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the defence electronics / order-book-driven compounder archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Defence electronics / order-book driven compounder
```

## Why BEL Requires Different Treatment

BEL should not be valued like a generic manufacturing company. Its valuation depends on defence order inflow, execution timelines, government customer concentration, indigenisation, electronics content, margin sustainability, cash conversion, and premium market expectations.

The model therefore emphasizes order-book execution, margin durability, working capital, and valuation-expectation risk.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. defence order inflow,
2. order-book execution,
3. government-customer concentration,
4. margin sustainability,
5. working-capital and cash-conversion risk,
6. capex intensity,
7. WACC and terminal growth,
8. premium valuation expectations.
