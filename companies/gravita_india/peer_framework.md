# Gravita India Peer Framework

## Purpose

This file defines the relative-valuation peer framework for Gravita India. It is not a final peer valuation table. It is a Level 2 framework that will later support SSRN-stage peer multiple refresh and citation work.

## Business Context

Gravita India operates in recycling and non-ferrous metal processing, with exposure to lead recycling, aluminium, plastics, rubber, overseas plants, circular-economy themes, and commodity-linked industrial demand.

Because the company combines structural recycling growth with commodity-cycle exposure, the peer set should include recycling companies, non-ferrous metal processors, commodity-linked industrials, and circular-economy / waste-management businesses where relevant.

## Indicative Peer Buckets

| Peer Bucket | Relevance | Example Peer Types |
|---|---|---|
| Recycling and circular-economy companies | Closest thematic comparison | Listed recycling, waste-processing, and circular-economy businesses |
| Non-ferrous metal processors | Operating and commodity-cycle comparison | Lead, aluminium, zinc, and other non-ferrous industrial processors |
| Commodity-linked industrials | Cycle and working-capital comparison | Industrial companies exposed to commodity prices and inventory cycles |
| High-growth industrial compounders | Valuation-quality comparison | Companies with capacity expansion, export growth, and strong ROCE expectations |

## Multiples to Track Later

| Multiple | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| EV / Sales | Useful when margins are commodity-sensitive |
| EV / EBITDA | Main operating valuation multiple for recycling and industrial peers |
| P / E | Captures market expectations for earnings growth and quality |
| P / B | Useful where working capital and balance-sheet intensity matter |
| ROCE and ROE | Helps judge whether premium multiples are supported by returns |
| Revenue CAGR | Helps compare growth durability across peers |
| EBITDA margin | Helps compare product mix and spread resilience |
| Cash conversion | Important for commodity-linked working-capital businesses |

## Peer-Selection Caution

A strict peer comparison may be difficult because Gravita has a combination of recycling, commodity-linked spreads, overseas operations, circular-economy positioning, and growth characteristics. Peer valuation should therefore be used as a secondary benchmark, not as the primary valuation method.

## SSRN Upgrade Requirement

During the SSRN stage, this framework should be upgraded with:

1. exact peer list,
2. consistent data date,
3. market capitalization,
4. enterprise value,
5. revenue, EBITDA, PAT,
6. EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/B,
7. ROCE, ROE,
8. cash-conversion metrics,
9. source citations.

## Research Boundary

This peer framework is for research workflow design only. It is not investment advice or a final relative valuation conclusion.
