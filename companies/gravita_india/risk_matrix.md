# Gravita India Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix documents the key business, financial, valuation, and data risks that affect the Gravita India probabilistic DCF model.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Valuation Impact | Monte Carlo Variable Affected | Current Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commodity price cycle | Lead and non-ferrous metal prices can affect revenue, spreads, margins and working capital | Changes revenue, margin and cash conversion | Revenue growth, EBIT margin, NWC / sales | Captured through growth, margin and NWC ranges | Level 2 draft |
| Recycling volume growth | Growth depends on collection network, processing capacity and demand for recycled metals | Changes revenue scale and operating leverage | Revenue growth | Captured through revenue-growth range | Level 2 draft |
| Value-added mix | Higher share of value-added products can improve margins and reduce commodity sensitivity | Supports margin durability and valuation quality | EBIT margin | Captured through margin distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Overseas operations | International plants can support growth but add FX, execution and country-risk exposure | Changes growth and risk profile | Revenue growth, WACC | Captured through CSRP and qualitative risk notes | Level 2 draft |
| Capacity expansion | Recycling growth may require capacity additions and project execution | Higher capex can reduce near-term FCFF but support future growth | Capex / sales, revenue growth | Captured through capex distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Working capital | Inventory and receivables can absorb cash in commodity-linked businesses | Reduces FCFF | NWC / sales | Captured through NWC distribution | Level 2 draft |
| Regulatory and environmental compliance | Recycling businesses face environmental, sourcing and compliance requirements | Can affect cost, capex and WACC | WACC, capex / sales, EBIT margin | Captured through CSRP and qualitative risk notes | Level 2 draft |
| Premium valuation risk | Market price may imply longer circular-economy growth and better cash conversion than base DCF | Creates gap between market price and DCF distribution | Market comparison | Probability-of-undervaluation calculated | Level 2 draft |
| Data quality | Public-source data definitions may differ from annual-report line items | Can affect all outputs | All variables | Final input table documents source status | Level 2 draft |

## Key Research Point

Gravita India is a useful recycling and circular-economy case because structural growth can coexist with commodity-cycle and working-capital risk. The DCF must test whether the market price already discounts a long growth runway and improved cash conversion.

## Research Boundary

This risk matrix is for research practice and model transparency. It is not an investment recommendation.
