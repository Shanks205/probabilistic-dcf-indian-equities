## Company Role in the Research Sample

Gravita India is included as the recycling / commodity-linked industrial / circular-economy case. It adds a different uncertainty profile because its valuation depends on recycling volume growth, lead and non-ferrous metal cycles, value-added product mix, overseas operations, capacity expansion, working-capital discipline, regulatory execution, and circular-economy valuation expectations.

The research value of Gravita is that it helps test whether a structurally attractive recycling business can justify a premium market valuation under a probabilistic FCFF framework while still carrying commodity-cycle and cash-conversion risk.

## Interpretation

The Level 2 draft model shows that Gravita India's market price is materially above the conservative intrinsic-value distribution generated by the model. This does not automatically mean the business is weak. It means the market price may be discounting stronger assumptions than the current base-case FCFF framework, including a longer recycling growth runway, higher value-added mix, superior commodity-spread management, stronger overseas execution, better cash conversion, or a longer circular-economy competitive advantage period.

The company is useful for the project because it shows how a structurally attractive circular-economy business can still carry commodity-cycle and valuation-expectation risk. A probabilistic DCF framework helps separate operating growth from the price already being paid for that growth.
