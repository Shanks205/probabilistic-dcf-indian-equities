# Gravita India — Company 12

## Role in the Project

Gravita India is the twelfth company in the probabilistic DCF valuation project. It represents the recycling / commodity-linked industrial / circular-economy archetype.

## Valuation Archetype

```text
Recycling / commodity-linked industrial / circular-economy platform
```

## Why Gravita Requires Different Treatment

Gravita should not be valued like a stable branded consumer or pure manufacturing company. Its valuation depends on recycling volumes, lead and non-ferrous metal prices, value-added product mix, overseas operations, capacity expansion, working-capital discipline, commodity-cycle volatility, regulatory execution, and circular-economy growth expectations.

The model therefore emphasizes revenue growth durability, margin sustainability, commodity sensitivity, capex intensity, working-capital absorption, WACC, terminal growth, and valuation-expectation risk.

## Main Uncertainty Drivers

The Monte Carlo model focuses on:

1. recycling volume growth,
2. lead and non-ferrous metal price cycle,
3. value-added product mix,
4. overseas operations and export contribution,
5. margin sustainability,
6. capacity expansion and capex intensity,
7. working-capital intensity,
8. regulatory and environmental-compliance risk,
9. WACC and terminal growth,
10. circular-economy premium valuation expectations.
