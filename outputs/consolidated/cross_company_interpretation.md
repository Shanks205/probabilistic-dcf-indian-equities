# Cross-Company Valuation Interpretation

## Purpose

This note summarizes the Level 2 cross-company valuation output for the 12-company Indian equity sample. It is designed to support the SSRN working paper's empirical results and discussion sections.

## Consolidated Observation

Across the 12-company sample, the conservative Level 2 probabilistic FCFF framework generally produces fair-value distributions below the market prices used in the draft analysis. This does not automatically imply that every company is overvalued or that market prices must decline. Instead, the result indicates that market prices may be embedding assumptions that are stronger than the conservative base-case DCF framework.

## Interpretation by Archetype

### 1. Mature cash-flow recovery

Hero MotoCorp has the smallest valuation gap in the sample. This suggests that a mature company with clearer cash-flow visibility may be less sensitive to extreme growth expectations than high-multiple growth or quality-compounder businesses.

### 2. Cyclical and working-capital-sensitive businesses

NALCO, Sharda Cropchem, Time Technoplast, and Gravita India show the importance of normalized margins, commodity cycles, cash conversion, and working-capital discipline. For these companies, a single-year profit base may not be representative of normalized long-term earning power.

### 3. Order-book and capex-cycle businesses

Triveni Turbine, Bharat Electronics, and Shilchar Technologies demonstrate that order-book visibility and structural capex themes can support strong business narratives, but conservative DCF models still need to test whether market prices already discount long growth runways.

### 4. Quality compounders and premiumization businesses

Polycab India, SJS Enterprises, and JB Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals highlight the key distinction between business quality and valuation-expectation risk. A business can have strong brands, high margins, durable demand, or premiumization tailwinds while still appearing expensive under conservative FCFF assumptions.

## Core Research Finding

The strongest finding from the Level 2 GitHub-stage work is not a buy/sell conclusion. The strongest finding is methodological:

```text
Probabilistic DCF is useful because it shows whether the current market price is supported by conservative cash-flow assumptions or whether it requires more optimistic growth, margin, risk, or terminal-value assumptions.
```
